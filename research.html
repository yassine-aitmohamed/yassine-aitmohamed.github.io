Algebra

  • Y. Ait Mohamed. On graded trivial extensions with homogeneous derivations. Submitted.
    Let $G$ be an arbitrary group, $R = \bigoplus_{\tau \in G} R_\tau$ a $G$-graded ring, and $M$ a graded $R$-bimodule. We show that every homogeneous derivation on the graded trivial extension $R \ltimes M$ decomposes canonically into four component maps, a graded analogue of a result of Mokhtari–Moafian–Vishki. We introduce the notion of a $\delta$-gr-simple ring and prove a graded analogue of Posner's theorem in prime characteristic.
  • Y. Ait Mohamed. Graded differential polynomial rings. Submitted.
    [PDF]
    Let $R$ be a group-graded ring and $\delta$ a derivation of $R$. We characterize when the differential polynomial ring $R[t;\delta]$ admits a compatible grading. We establish graded analogues of the classical simplicity theorems of Jordan and of Öinert–Silvestrov. We also show that gr-primality and gr-Noetherianity each transfer from $R$ to $R[t;\delta]$.
  • Y. Ait Mohamed. Generalized homogeneous derivations on graded rings. Siberian Mathematical Journal. To appear.
    We introduce a notion of generalized homogeneous derivations on graded rings as a natural extension of the homogeneous derivations defined by Kanunnikov. Several significant results originally established for prime rings are extended to the setting of gr-prime rings, and we characterize conditions under which gr-semiprime rings contain nontrivial central graded ideals.
  • Y. Ait Mohamed. On graded rings with homogeneous derivations. Revista de la Unión Matemática Argentina. Published.
    [Journal]
    We establish results related to homogeneous derivations, a concept introduced by Kanunnikov (2018). We extend certain existing significant results in the context of prime (resp. semiprime) rings to gr-prime (resp. gr-semiprime) rings, such as Posner's and Herstein's theorems.
