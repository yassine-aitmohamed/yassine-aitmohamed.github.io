  1. Shifted Lagrangian geometry of curves in Radko surfaces (with Maxence Mayrand)
    We describe the shifted Lagrangian geometry of curves in Radko surfaces, through their global integrations and their homotopy Poisson algebras. We construct the adjoint symplectic groupoid of the surface explicitly, show that admissible curves integrate to embedded Lagrangian subgroupoids, which are 1-shifted Lagrangians, and characterize when their shifted intersections are clean. For a component of the degeneracy locus, a canonical Hamiltonian flow on its shifted self-intersection recovers the modular period. On the infinitesimal side, the homotopy Poisson algebra of a connected admissible curve not contained in the degeneracy locus is $L_\infty$-isomorphic to its underlying cochain complex, and its $L_\infty$-isomorphism class is determined by the total contact multiplicity. The brackets of arity at least two can be removed by a change of tubular neighbourhood exactly when all contacts are transverse. At tangential contacts, the binary bracket retains the modular vector field. We establish this distinction using a general characterization of the $L_\infty$-isomorphisms induced by changes of tubular neighbourhood, valid for arbitrary coisotropic submanifolds.
  2. Dual reflection groups of Ore extensions and McKay quivers (with Asmae El-Baz)
    In preparation
    Let $B=A[t;\sigma,\delta]$ be a graded Ore extension, where $\sigma$ is an automorphism. We prove that if $B$ is Artin–Schelter Gorenstein or regular, then so is $A$. We use this to determine the dual reflection groups of $B$ for gradings compatible with the Ore structure. If $\delta\neq0$, they are the dual reflection groups of $A$ whose components are preserved by $\sigma$ and $\delta$. If $\delta=0$, they are semidirect products of a dual reflection group of $A$ by the cyclic group generated by the degree of $t$. For iterated Ore extensions, these groups are solvable, the conjecture of Buzaglo and Rogalski on binomial relations holds, and the covariant ring is a complete intersection, which answers a question of Kirkman, Kuzmanovich and Zhang. Finally, we show that the McKay quiver of the coaction is the Cayley quiver of the group with respect to the degrees of the variables, with relations, and that the smash product of $B$ with the dual group algebra becomes a matrix algebra over the identity component once the Jacobian is inverted.
  3. Integration and quantization of 1-shifted coisotropics (with Maxence Mayrand)
    Submitted · pdf
    To quantize the reduction of a coisotropic submanifold of a Poisson manifold, Cattaneo and Felder constructed a homotopy Poisson algebra together with a deformation quantization. Its degree-zero cohomology is the reduced Poisson algebra, while the full homotopy structure retains information about the formal embedding even when the reduced space is singular or trivial. On the integration side, Cattaneo showed that a coisotropic submanifold integrates to a Lagrangian subgroupoid of any symplectic groupoid integrating the ambient Poisson manifold. It is now recognized that both constructions fit into shifted symplectic geometry: the Lagrangian subgroupoid presents a 1-shifted Lagrangian morphism of differentiable stacks, and the homotopy Poisson algebra describes the associated 0-shifted Poisson structure. However, such morphisms form only a special class of 1-shifted Lagrangians. We extend this picture to 1-shifted Lagrangians and, more generally, 1-shifted coisotropics in 1-shifted symplectic differentiable stacks. We establish an integration theorem for their infinitesimal description in terms of twisted Dirac structures and construct a natural Poisson bracket on invariant functions. Under regularity hypotheses, we equip the associated Lie algebroid complex with a flat $P_\infty$-structure and a curved $A_\infty$-quantization, independent of the auxiliary choices up to isomorphism. The induced bracket in degree zero recovers the reduced Poisson bracket, and suitable cohomological vanishing conditions yield a deformation quantization of invariant functions. This extends the Cattaneo–Felder picture to more general reduction procedures, with the same infinitesimal data governing integration and quantization.
  4. Group-graded Ore extensions and graded simplicity
    Submitted · pdf
    We study Ore extensions $R[t;\sigma,\delta]$ of rings graded by an arbitrary group $\Gamma$. The grading of $R$ extends with $t$ of degree $\gamma$ if and only if $\sigma$ is compatible with conjugation by $\gamma$ and $\delta$ with left translation by $\gamma$. The extended grading is then unique. For nonabelian $\Gamma$ the degree of $t$ need not commute with the degrees of $R$, and this twist cannot always be removed. If $R$ is gr-simple and $\sigma$ is an automorphism, then $R[t;\sigma,\delta]$ is gr-simple if and only if it has no homogeneous normal element that is monic of positive $t$-degree. The quantized Weyl algebra shows that the graded forms of the classical necessary conditions do not suffice. Over a group algebra $kG$, these extensions correspond to actions of $G$ on the affine line, and gr-simplicity means that the action has no finite orbit. In characteristic zero this is the nonvanishing of a class in $H^1(G,k_\chi)$, and simplicity forces $G$ to be metabelian. We prove the analogous results for Poisson polynomial rings and relate the two settings by semiclassical limits.
  5. Graded differential polynomial rings
    Mathematische Nachrichten · Published · pdf
    We study differential polynomial rings $R[t;\delta]$ over $\Gamma$-graded rings, where $\Gamma$ is an arbitrary group. We show that $R[t;\delta]$ admits a $\Gamma$-grading compatible with that of $R$ if and only if $\delta$ is a $\gamma$-derivation for some $\gamma\in C_\Gamma(\Gamma_R)$, and that this grading is unique once $\deg(t)=\gamma$ is fixed; if $\delta\neq0$, then $\gamma$ is itself uniquely determined by $\delta$. We characterize the resulting graded ring by a universal property. We prove a characteristic-free center criterion for gr-simplicity whenever $Z(R[t;\delta])$ is a graded subring; in characteristic zero, gr-simplicity is equivalent to $\delta$-gr-simplicity of $R$ and $\gamma$-outerness of $\delta$, extending Jordan's simplicity criterion to the graded setting. We further show that $R[t;\delta]$ is gr-prime if and only if $R$ is $\delta$-gr-prime, and that gr-Noetherianity of $R$ passes to $R[t;\delta]$, recovering a graded Hilbert basis theorem as a special case. When $\Gamma$ is abelian, gr-simplicity and gr-primality are shown to be invariants of homogeneous graded Morita equivalence, and every ring homogeneously graded equivalent to $R[t;\delta]$ via a compatible idempotent is again a graded differential polynomial ring.
  6. Generalized homogeneous derivations on graded rings
    Siberian Mathematical Journal · To appear · pdf
    We introduce a notion of generalized homogeneous derivations on graded rings, extending the homogeneous derivations of Kanunnikov, and single out the gr-generalized derivations among them, those preserving the degree of every homogeneous component. Several results originally established for prime rings are extended to gr-prime rings. We also study the algebraic and module-theoretic structure of these maps, establish their functorial properties, and construct categories that record their derivation structure in both ring and module contexts.
  7. On graded rings with homogeneous derivations
    Revista de la Unión Matemática Argentina · Published · pdf
    We establish results related to homogeneous derivations, a concept introduced by Kanunnikov (2018). First, we prove the existence of a non-trivial family of derivations that are not homogeneous on graded rings. Furthermore, based on homogeneous derivations, we extend certain existing significant results in the context of prime (resp. semiprime) rings to gr-prime (resp. gr-semiprime) rings, such as Posner's and Herstein's theorems.
