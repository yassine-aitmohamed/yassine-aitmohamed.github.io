I am a PhD candidate in Mathematics at the University of Sherbrooke, Canada, under the supervision of Prof. Maxence Mayrand.

Prior to this, I was a PhD student at the University of Sidi Mohamed Ben Abdellah, Morocco, supervised by Prof. Lahcen Oukhtite.

I completed my Master's in Mathematics in 2022 at the University of Sidi Mohamed Ben Abdellah. My master's thesis focused on Divisors in Algebraic Geometry, Central Simple Algebras and Severi–Brauer Varieties.

Research Interests

My research interests lie at the intersection of algebra and geometry. More specifically, they are organized around the following directions:

Algebra: Derivations theory on graded rings; homogeneous and generalized homogeneous derivations; differential polynomial rings and their gr-simplicity; graded trivial extensions; Posner-type and Herstein-type theorems for gr-prime and gr-semiprime rings.

Poisson & Symplectic Geometry: Poisson structures on shifted coisotropics in the framework of derived algebraic geometry; shifted symplectic geometry; topological quantum field theories; Lie groups and their geometric representations.

Algebraic Geometry: Divisors and line bundles; central simple algebras and the Brauer group; Severi–Brauer varieties; sheaf theory.
